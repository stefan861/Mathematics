## Manuscript catalogue

### 1. [A Measurable Bicycle Theorem](bicycle.pdf)

**Areas:** geometric combinatorics; graph colouring; measurable colourings; unit-distance geometry.

**Summary:** Establishes a measurable analogue of the bicycle theorem for two unit circles whose centres are at a distance strictly between 1 and 2. It rules out weak measurable 3-colourings on every open neighbourhood of the two circles, and extends the result to sufficiently small (C^1) perturbations of the circles. The proof develops integer step counts, shell and cone lemmas, and a removability argument for the set where all three colours accumulate.

**Scope note:** The manuscript presents this as one step toward a measurable Sokolov–Voronov-type argument. It explicitly does **not** claim to resolve the Hadwiger–Nelson problem.

### 2. [Extremality of Truncated Bergman Fans in Nef Cones of Permutohedral Varieties](truncated_bergman.pdf)

**Areas:** matroid theory; algebraic combinatorics; tropical geometry; algebraic geometry; nef cones.

**Summary:** Studies when truncated Bergman fans span extremal rays of nef cones on permutohedral varieties. The manuscript gives a classification for one-dimensional truncations, reduces higher-dimensional extremality to uniqueness (up to scaling) of solutions of an explicit linear system on flats of specified ranks, provides sufficient conditions for extremality and non-extremality, and constructs an infinite family of non-extremal examples from the graphic matroids of (K_{2,m}). It also reports exhaustive computations for loopless matroids on at most seven elements.
